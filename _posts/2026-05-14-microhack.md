2026 brings the 5th continuation of the Microcontroller Hackathon. Members of UQ MARS and QUT robotics now have to tackle the theme of games. Participants could either build some sort of game, or anything else that is gamified. Students had access to a pool of parts which included ESP32 microcontrollers (sponsored by expressif), LEDs, Motors, TFT, OLED and E-INK displays, various sensors (infrared, in).
In addition, 3D printing was available as a service, which saw use to create various creative housings and mechanical components.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2026/MH-1.jpg"%}

Saturday was the big grind day, where many students found themselves deep in code, 3d modelling and tons of rounds of debugging. Executives were around to help out and take a look at all the interesting projects around.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2026/MH-5.jpg"%}


On the final day, a pitching workshop was held as students finalised their projects and did a 5 minute presentation in front of all the judges and participants.

### Participating Teams
- Airshott; Using your fingers for an arcade game
- AmongUs IRL; Completing tasks on physical game devices
- Adriel (laser scanner); Laser scanning with an $80 Sensor
- iDrinkSpeed; Drinking game to keep you hydrated
- iSuck; A continuation of iBin, ISuck is an Ibis shaped Vaccum controlled by a wii-remote
- Simon; Simon says?
- Musical Chairs; but the chairs have motors and run away from you
- Plane spotting; A spotter that points towards the closest available airliner
- Project Hail Mary; Turbo TV gets its physical form (like a Tamagotchi)!
- Simulating VR Recoil; VR Gunstock with haptic recoil
- Still on a watchlist; Bomb defusal game
- Team Lorax; A plant watching game
- Chronobox; A plethora of games all in one cube

### Prize Winners
- 1st - Project Hail Mary 🏆 
- 2nd - iSuck 
- 3rd - among us irl 
- Most Masochistic - Chronobox
- Best First Years - SIMON
- People's Choice  - Project Hail Mary

We thank the co-hosts and volunteers, as well as all the participants over the weekend.
