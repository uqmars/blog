2024 saw a pivot in the existing event, with the rebranding from the "Arduino Hackathon" to the "Microcontroller Hackathon". It also marked a return to form, bringing the event back to UQ's St Lucia campus.
This year challenged participants to address the theme of 'Innovations in Education', tying the event back to the roots of the Arduino Hackathon as a vessel to innovate on the outreach offerings of Robogals, in a send off marking the final event of Robogals Brisbane before the chapter was brought to an end.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2024/MH-6.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="Group photos of all participating teams" %}

The weekend opened as it tends to with tickets check-ins and participant setup, allowing those in attendance to connect and make some early headway on forming teams. This was followed by a migration over to the opening ceremony where the contestants were briefed on how the weekend would operate.

Following the opening ceremony and the subsequent dinner offering, participants got underway brainstorming and prototyping their early ideas. This was enhanced by this year's event sponsors, [Espressif Systems](https://www.espressif.com/), [Micromelon Robotics](https://www.micromelon.com.au/), and [MakerHero](https://makerhero.com.au/).

The Saturday's schedule was entirely up to the teams to develop as they pleased, with the optional workshops on microcontroller programming, electronic skills, and CAD running as per past years to help build up those who newer to working with mechatronic systems.

The Sunday saw the final polishing of ideas, with teams racing to implement any final ideas and embed them into their presentation prior to the submission deadline. Teams were also provided with a final workshop on pitching and presenting, featuring the return of [Simeon Gover](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simeongover/) who helped introduce the workshop in 2023.

The afternoon then saw teams refine and practice their pitches in preparation for the evening's presentations to the judging panel, comprising a selection of former members / executive from each of the three hosting associations:
- [Tim Hadwen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tim-hadwen-53863185/), representative from Micromelon Robotics and UQ MARS' first life member
- [Wilson Kong](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wilson-kong-2290ba83/), previously invovled through both Robogals and former collaborator [Robotics Playground](https://www.linkedin.com/company/roboticspg/)
- [Harry Stone](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stoneharry/), former QUTRC exec and past participant

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2024/MH-4.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="Prize trophies on display" %}

To kick off the presentations came the surprise announcement that all prizes on offer were to be doubled in value.
This meant that the following prizes were up for grabs:
- First Overall (\$500 -> \$1000)
- Runner-Up (\$300 -> \$600)
- Best First-Years (\$50 -> \$100)
- Best Presentation (\$50 -> \$100)
- Creative Use of Resources (\$50 -> \$100)
- People's Choice (\$50 -> \$100)
- Masochist Handshake (A firm handshake from the president of UQ MARS commending your efforts; \$1 -> \$2)

First Place was won by *PID Pilot*, reigning champions after their victory in 2023 as *Reversiball*. 
They presented an idea for an educational tool to help build an intuition behind a PID controller for those new to control systems.
The repository for their project can be found [here](https://github.com/Negativentropy/Hackathon2024).

The Runner-Up prize was given to the team *Omnes Astutus*.
They presented an idea for a Handheld Education Assistant System designed to increase interactivity in the classroom.
The repository for their project can be found [here](https://github.com/Sadteeto/Hackathon).

The other prizes were given to the following teams:
- People's Choice and Best Presentation: [Lick Batteries](https://github.com/tinaz7/MEWS)
- Best First-Years: [The Phreekie Fonuhks](https://github.com/DonnaT0207/Phonics-game)
- Creative Use of Resources: [The Pagans](https://github.com/UltraFishy/Project_Hermes)
- Masochist Handshake: [Team JAFS](https://github.com/arekuchan/MechChessAI)
