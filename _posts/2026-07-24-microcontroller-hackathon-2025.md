2025 introduced the event to a new location and setting while keeping the essentials change to Hackathon with it being hosted at QUT Garden’s Point. Students from UQ MARS and QUT Robotics were brought together under one roof to create a product within a 3 day time-frame. Sadly this was the first year since 2022 where Robogals were not involved, however they were lived on through their equipment donations that were used by the students.

Keeping with tradition established in past years the weekend began with an opening ceremony where competitors were introduced to the this year’s theme being, Make it Better, when they were challenged to select a current existing solution and improve upon it in any which way they deemed fit. After the ceremony everyone was given time to socialise, network and discuss ideas to form teams and begin the early stages. 

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2025/MH-1.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="Opening ceremony and theme reveal" %}

Saturday left it mostly in the hands of the competitors, to begin physically producing their idea. QUT Robotics had a workspace and were kind enough to open it to the competitors, with an induction of course. It was exciting as it gave them a space with tools and some hardware which was able to be used. In collaboration with that, continuing from last years introduction of 3D printing station, students were able to submit their designs for them to be brought to life by the printers. At the end of the day there were many tired faces, but through this lots of progress was being made and ideas coming to fruition. However, much dread for the inevitable all-nighters and final push to submit their idea and presentations for the next day.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2025/MH-2.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="Contestants discussing and planning" %}

Feedback from 2024 was used to streamline the workshops that were presented for students, with the main one being the public speaking workshop, which showed the most interest. This is how Sunday morning began with students arriving early, and some who may have never left, attending the public speaking workshop to prepare themselves for the presentations. Along with this, there was a sense of urgency but accomplishment around the competition as people began finalizing their designs and putting in the final touches, with all their hard work paying off.

After submitting, the weekend then led to the big final moment, the presentations. This saw many amazing, wonderful ideas come to life along with very entertaining and informative showings. In which the Following prizes were up for grabs:
- WINNER (\$500)
- RUNNER-UP (\$300)
- BEST FIRST-YEARS (\$50)
- BEST PRESENTATION (\$50)
- CREATIVE USE OF RESOURCES (\$50)
- PEOPLE’S CHOICE (\$50)
- MASOCHIST HANDSHAKE (\$1)

The First Place prize was awarded to Baby Got Back with their product ‘Form Fit’, in which their idea was an ergonomic desk chair that was electric and automatically formed to the shape of a person’s back. This would happen over time and slowly correct ones back shape which would be tailored to their posture improvement plan.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2025/MH-3.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="Winning team, Baby Got Back, with their winning project" %}

UQ MARS would like to thank all event sponsors for their contributions to the event: UQ Robotics for allowing us to use their facilities and equipment and for co-hosting and co-organising the event, the helpers who gave up their time to be here and help out with the event, as well as everyone who took the time over their weekend to participate in the event.
