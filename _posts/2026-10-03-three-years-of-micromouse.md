As the UQ Micromouse competition comes to a close for 2026, it is worth taking a step back to reflect on the history of the event over the last three years, from its debut at the end of 2023 through to now.

After our teams went to compete in the [Micromouse competition at TechFest]({% post_url 2020-02-03-techfest-micromouse %}), there was great ambition to bring it home and provide the opportunity for the local Brisbane community to bring it to life. Unfortunately, [certain events](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic) made this a pipe dream for a number of years, but the hope and aspirations lived on over the years and between the Executive Teams, eventually spawning the first rendition of the event in December of 2023.

This first event ran in the UQ Innovate Makerspace across the 7th and 8th of Decemember in 2023, joined by our frequent event collaborators of the [IEEE HKN Mu-Kappa chapter](https://hkn.ieee.org/hkn-chapters/all-chapters/mu-kappa-chapter), paying homage to the event's roots. The event was broken into the first day for testing / calibration with the competition maze, and the second day of competition. At the conclusion of the event we named the inaugural victor of the UQ Micromouse competition, Zac Apelt, though the maze remained undefeated with no competitors managing to reach its centre.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2026/MM-2023.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="2023 competition event organisers" %}

The following year, a decision was made to push the event back to a February date to align better with competitors being able to use their summer university breaks to further develop their solutions. As such, the second event did not take place until the 15th and 16th of February in 2025 (meaning that there was no 2024 Micromouse Competition). This event would also be the first to be led by a previous competitor, with Zac Apelt serving as our Competitions Lead and event organiser by this stage. The competitions saw even more mice zipping about, seeing a team actually reach the centre. The competition was this year was taken out by team the team 'Evangelion Weebs', comprising Kristopher Dickson, Riku Oya, Jack Crans, and Mikayla Dickinson.

{% include figure.html img_path="/assets/images/2026/MM-2025.jpg" alt_txt="" caption="2025 competition event organisers and competitors" %}

In 2026, we saw a change in tact, with the first offerings of the Micromouse workshops, and the associated starter kits being available for purchase. This change ultimately saw a marked improvement of the average system, but also a great drop in the diversity of entrants. This year we've also seen some variations in competition timings, with two competitions being offered in the one year, one in the same February period as 2025, and the recent one in October. The respective winners of these comps were:
- Lab Rats in semester one, comprised of Srijit Mukherjee and Raymond Li
- Speedy Gonzales in semester two, comprised of Lucas Hennig and Shiyendri Jayatilake
